# Night Swing

A comic-book night-city theme for the Claude Code desktop app. A masked web-slinging hero patrols a moonlit harbor behind your conversation, and the scene follows what Claude is doing:

- **While Claude works**, the hero swings into one of a few jobs: a brawl with a four-armed scientist on top of the bridge tower, a bank robbery chase across the bridge, or a grinning bomber on a glider. A long job gets a change of scenery every few steps.
- **When Claude asks you something**, the hero hangs upside down from the bridge cable and waits for your call.
- **When Claude is done**, the hero lands on a rooftop in close-up ("Mission complete!") and the villain hangs webbed up under the bridge.
- **When a step fails**, danger-sense lines flash around the hero's head.

The city has a stone suspension bridge with moving traffic, a river full of reflections, a ferry, searchlights and a halftone sky. Messages get a comic look too: yellow caption boxes for yours, inked panels for Claude's, and bold comic headings. Dark mode only.

## Extras

- `/spidey` gives you a random quip from the hero.
- Press the speaker icon in the session's title bar for sound: a quiet city hum, a *thwip* on each tool call, a fanfare when Claude lands, and a tingle when it asks you something.

## Install

At the prompt of a Claude Code terminal session:

```text
/plugin install night-swing --marketplace dmolloy98-stack-stack/claude_themes
```

Answer `y` to add the marketplace, then press Enter to install for your user. It loads in every new session on that computer, the desktop app included.

From a shell:

```bash
claude plugin install night-swing --marketplace dmolloy98-stack-stack/claude_themes
```

In the desktop app: click **+** next to the prompt box, then **Plugins**, **Add marketplace** with `dmolloy98-stack-stack/claude_themes`, then **Add plugin**.

Start a new session afterwards and the city appears behind the conversation.

## A note on inspiration

This is a just-for-fun fan project. It takes its mood from classic comic books and old browser superhero games, but everything here is drawn from scratch: the hero, the villains and the city are original artwork, and no official characters, logos or art are used. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any comic publisher or studio, and it isn't meant to copy anyone's work.
